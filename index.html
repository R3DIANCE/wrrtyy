Maple Wayfarer

A fan-made MapleStory-style platformer · WebGPU + Bun

  • ← → Move
  • Alt / C / Space Jump
  • ↑ ↓ Ladders, portals, prone
  • Ctrl / X Attack
  • Shift / A Slash Blast
  • Z Loot
  • ↓ + Jump Drop down
  • 1 / 2 Potions
